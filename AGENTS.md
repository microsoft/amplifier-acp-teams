feature, release or gap inventory. Admin pages do not define future product UX.

within authority needs no new approval.

[validation](docs/validation.md) and applicable CI. Distinguish fixture evidence
from real platform authorization, delivery and interaction quality. Keep credentials,
transcripts, installation details and private experiments outside published source.
